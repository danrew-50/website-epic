Common Thread.

A digital system built around connection, rhythm and generous space.
The idea

The concept turns one repeated graphic gesture into a navigation device. It leads the eye through the site, giving the digital experience a memorable movement without relying on heavy effects.

01 / Interface composition
02 / Campaign moment
03 / Responsive language
Approach
A system that moves without showing off.

Motion is used to clarify changes of state, guide attention and add rhythm to long-form pages.

The foundation remains typographic, editorial and highly usable so the experience holds together when motion is reduced or removed.
